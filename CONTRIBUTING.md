# Contributing to Convert2Print

Thank you for your interest in Convert2Print!

Convert2Print is an independently developed platform focused on professional print preparation, digital artwork workflows, and accessible creative tools.

Our goal is to build useful technology and a welcoming community for digital artists, developers, designers, and independent creators.

## How You Can Contribute

You don't need to be a programmer to contribute.

We welcome:

- **Ideas and feature suggestions** for Convert2Print and Creative Studio.
- **Bug reports and feedback** about the public platform.
- **Documentation improvements** that help users understand professional print preparation.
- **Workflow suggestions** for digital artists, Etsy sellers, print-on-demand businesses, and other creators.
- **Community discussions** about print technology, digital products, and creative software.

## Where to Participate

Please use our [GitHub Discussions](https://github.com/convert2print/convert2print-community/discussions) to share ideas, ask questions, and discuss improvements.

You can also follow our [Public Development Roadmap](https://github.com/users/convert2print/projects/1) to see current priorities and planned features.

## About the Golden Pipeline

Convert2Print is powered by the Golden Pipeline, a proprietary, deterministic processing engine.

The engine does not use generative AI for artwork processing.

Its source code is not publicly distributed, and this repository is intended for documentation, development updates, and community collaboration.

Contributions do not require access to the private processing engine.

## A Respectful Community

We encourage constructive feedback, respectful communication, and practical suggestions.

Please avoid spam, unsolicited advertising, and sharing confidential information.

## Supporting the Project

Convert2Print is independently developed in Denmark.

Voluntary support helps fund development, testing, infrastructure, documentation, and the long-term goal of making professional creative tools more accessible.

You can learn more about supporting the project through [GitHub Sponsors](https://github.com/sponsors/convert2print).

Support is entirely optional and is not required to participate.
