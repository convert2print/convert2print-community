## What Is Convert2Print?

Convert2Print develops tools for professional artwork preparation and digital print production.

The existing processing technology supports:

- Professional image resizing and scaling
- Color management and embedded ICC profiles
- JPG and PDF/X-4 output
- Print-ready file preparation
- Multiple print sizes and aspect ratios
- Batch processing and automated workflows
- Print preflight and quality analysis

The platform uses deterministic image-processing methods rather than generative AI to creatively reinterpret existing artwork.

**The goal is to simplify complex technical workflows while preserving the original artwork.**

Visit the platform: https://convert2print.com

## Introducing Convert2Print Creative Studio

**One Free Studio. One Workspace. More Possibilities.**

Creative Studio is the next major development initiative within Convert2Print.

It is being designed as a free professional workspace for digital artists, illustrators, designers and digital product sellers worldwide.

Instead of switching between multiple applications, creators will be able to prepare, organize, manage, export and present their artwork in one place.

### Milestone 1 – First Public Release

**Status: In Development**

The first release focuses on digital wall art and includes:

- Artwork Library
- Project Workspace
- Resize & Crop
- Professional Artwork Preparation
- Print-Ready Downloads
- Export Center
- Mockup Studio

Several features are already available in a local working preview. Other components are still being developed and tested.

### Milestone 2 – Digital Product Creation Tools

**Status: Planned**

Following the first public release, development will expand toward additional creative tools, including:

- SVG and vector artwork
- Printable stickers
- Digital planners
- Creative templates
- Additional digital product formats
- Advanced export workflows

The long-term vision is to support many different types of digital creators within one accessible workspace.
